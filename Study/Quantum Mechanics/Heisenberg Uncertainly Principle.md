$$\sigma^2_{A} = \langle \hat{Q}^2_{A}\rangle -\langle \hat{Q}_{A}\rangle^2$$
$$\sigma^2_{B} = \langle \hat{Q}^2_{B}\rangle -\langle \hat{Q}_{B}\rangle^2$$
$$\implies \sigma^2_{A}\sigma^2_{B} \geq \left( \frac{1}{2i}\langle[\hat{A}, \hat{B}]\rangle \right)^2$$
$$\sigma_{x}\sigma_{p} \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}, \sigma_{E}\sigma_{t} \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
#### Độ lệch của phép đo
$$\begin{gather}
\langle \hat{Q}\rangle = \langle \Psi | \hat{Q}|\Psi\rangle \\
\hat{Q}|\Psi_{n}\rangle = Q_{n}|\Psi_{n}\rangle \\
\sigma^2 = \langle (\Delta Q )^2\rangle \, \Delta Q = \hat{Q} - \langle \hat{Q}\rangle \\
\sigma^2 = \langle (\hat{Q} - \langle Q \rangle)^2\rangle \, = \langle \hat{Q}^2 - 2\hat{Q}\langle \hat{Q}\rangle +\langle Q\rangle^2 \rangle \\
= \langle \hat{Q}^2\rangle - 2\langle \hat{Q}\rangle\langle \hat{Q}\rangle + \langle \hat{Q}\rangle^2 = \langle \hat{Q}^2\rangle - \langle \hat{Q}\rangle^2 \\
\sigma^2 = \langle (\hat{Q} - \langle \hat{Q}\rangle)^2 \rangle = \langle (\hat{Q} - \langle \hat{Q}\rangle)\Psi | (\hat{Q} - \langle \hat{Q}\rangle)\Psi\rangle
\end{gather}$$
Độ lệch chuẩn của một đại lượng đo được A
$$\sigma^2_{A} = \langle (\hat{A} - \langle A\rangle)\Psi | (\hat{A} - \langle A\rangle)\Psi\rangle = \langle f|f\rangle$$
Với 
$$f\equiv (\hat{A} - \langle A \rangle)\Psi$$
Sử dụng bất đẳng thức Schwarz
$$\left|\int^b_{a}f(x)^*g(x)\,dx\right| \leq \sqrt{ \int^b_{a}|f(x)|^2 \, dx \int^b_{a}|g(x)|^2 \, dx }$$
