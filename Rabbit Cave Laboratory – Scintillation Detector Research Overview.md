**1. Introduction**  
_Rabbit Cave Laboratory_ is a student-founded **nuclear research laboratory** focusing on the development and characterization of **radiation detectors**.  
The lab is currently working on **scintillation detectors** for applications in **radioactive source detection**, **gamma-ray energy spectroscopy**, **radiation monitoring**, and **cosmic ray observation**.  
Our studies aim to evaluate detector performance through light yield measurement, signal analysis, and practical implementation in radiation detection systems.
**2. Scintillation Detectors in Use**

The laboratory currently operates three main types of scintillation detectors, selected for different measurement purposes:

- **NaI(Tl) Detector** – Sodium Iodide doped with Thallium provides a **high light yield (~38,000 photons/MeV)** and emits light with a **peak wavelength around 415 nm**, well matched to typical bialkali photocathodes.  
    It is mainly used for **gamma-ray spectroscopy** and **energy calibration**, offering excellent energy resolution.
    
- **BGO Detector** – Bismuth Germanate has a **moderate light yield (~8,000–9,000 photons/MeV)** and emits at a **peak wavelength of about 480 nm**.  
    With its high density (**7.13 g/cm³**) and strong stopping power, it is ideal for **high-energy gamma detection** in compact setups.
    
- **Plastic Scintillator** – Based on polyvinyltoluene or polystyrene, it provides a **light yield of approximately 10,000 photons/MeV** with an emission **peak near 420 nm** and a **decay time of a few nanoseconds**.  
    It is used in **cosmic ray detection**, **muon counting**, and **coincidence timing** experiments due to its fast response and mechanical robustness.
    

**3. Research Activities**  
Rabbit Cave Lab is developing a **SiPM-based readout system** to replace conventional PMTs, offering compact size and high sensitivity to low-light signals.  
Our ongoing work focuses on accurate light yield characterization and pulse-shape analysis.  
Current research directions include:

- Comparing and calibrating **light yield and energy response** among NaI(Tl), BGO, and plastic scintillators.
    
- Constructing a **cosmic ray telescope** using multiple plastic scintillator layers for muon detection.
    
- Developing a **gamma-ray calibration setup** based on NaI(Tl) and BGO detectors.
    

**4. Purpose and Collaboration**  
The _Rabbit Cave Laboratory_ aims to provide students with hands-on research experience in detector physics, data acquisition, and scintillation analysis.
